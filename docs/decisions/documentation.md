Closed decisions about this repository's documents. The rule itself is in `AGENTS.md` ("Documentation languages"); this file keeps when it was decided and what came before.

## Japanese counterparts keep the English headings

- **Decision** (2026-11-12): a Japanese counterpart (`README.ja.md`, `docs/<name>.ja.md`, `docs/decisions/<subject>.ja.md`) has the headings of its English file, the same strings in the same order. Only the body is Japanese.
- **History**: `README.ja.md` had the English headings when English became the base language of the documents (2026-10-20, semnil/py-analyze-common#19). A pass that made the documents uniform turned them into Japanese (2026-11-06, semnil/py-analyze-common#51), and `docs/auto-update.ja.md` was added with Japanese headings (2026-11-07, semnil/py-analyze-common#55). The decision above put the six headings of `README.ja.md` and the nine of `docs/auto-update.ja.md` back.
